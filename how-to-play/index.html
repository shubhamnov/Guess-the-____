A round, step by step

  1. Pick a gameTap any card on the home page, or play today’s Daily Mix: one question from every game, the same for everyone that day.
  2. Answer fastEach question has four choices. A right answer is +10, a wrong one −5, and the clock keeps running. After a wrong answer the right one lights up.
  3. It gets harderThe first five questions are a warm-up. After that the wrong choices come from the same group, like flags from the same region or films with the same stars.
  4. Beat your bestYour best score and longest streak are saved on this device and show on each card.

More ways to play

⚡With a friend

Tap a game and choose With a friend. Send your friend the 4-letter code, or they type it in “Got a code?” on the home page. You both get the same questions and see each other’s score live. Two players per room.

📅Daily Mix

A new mix every day, one question from every game. Everyone with the same setting gets the same questions, so you can compare scores.

✍️Name Them All

A different kind of game: type every answer you can think of, like all the planets or every Shah Rukh Khan film, before the time runs out.

🌐India, Global or Both

Some games have Indian and international questions. Choose with the button at the top right of any page, or on the Settings page.

⌨️Keyboard

Keys 1–4 answer, Enter plays again and M turns sound on or off.

🔊Sound

Music and answer sounds can be turned off in any game, or on the Settings page.
